to its next damage and then seeking at least another 1.5x improvement by
changing producer handoff behavior.

its old timing premise is subject to the contaminated-evidence audit now
tracked by #121. The item is withdrawn rather than allowed to extend or repair
the current port. A later measured product goal may open a new specified
