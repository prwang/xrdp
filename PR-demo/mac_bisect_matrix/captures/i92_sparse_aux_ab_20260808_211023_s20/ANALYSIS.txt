=== THE RATE ===

  "frame interval" is the wall-clock gap between consecutive FRAMES
  handed to the transport (one egress record each) -- the same quantity
  e_gate_run.sh prints as its send-to-send gap, and it agrees with it
  to within 0.2 ms. "period" is pump_beg to pump_beg, one encoder worker
  cycle. "pump" is the part of that cycle spent waiting for the ffmpeg
  children to finish encoding -- the segment a second encode is paid in.
  "writes/frame" is how many network writes one frame took: a luma-only
  frame carries one PDU where a full frame carries two.
